What it is

JCMS Social Publisher is the name of the video publishing workflow operated by JCMS Digital Media. It is built on Postiz, an open-source social media scheduling tool that we run on our own infrastructure. We use it to organize finished videos and publish them to social accounts that the account holder has explicitly authorized.

Today the service is used to publish video content to TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not a public self-service signup product, and it does not publish anything to an account that has not been connected through an authorization that the account holder approved.

Current status. Facebook and Instagram are planned and are not available yet. This page will be updated when they are.

How it works

1

Prepare

Finished videos are edited outside the app and added to the publishing queue with their caption and settings.

2

Authorize

The account holder signs in on the platform's own page and approves access. We never see or store the account password.

3

Review

Each video is reviewed before it is sent. Nothing is sent to an account that is not connected.

4

Publish

The video is sent to the connected account through the platform's official API, as a draft to finish in the app or as a direct post, depending on the permission granted.

Account authorization

Accounts are connected only through the official authorization (OAuth) screen of each platform. For TikTok, the permissions requested are:

user.info.basic

Reads the account's display name and avatar so we can show which account is connected.

video.upload

Sends a video to the account as a draft, for the account holder to finish and post inside TikTok.

video.publish

Posts a video directly to the account when the account holder has approved it for publishing.

The account holder can disconnect an account at any time, in the connected account's own app-permissions settings or by contacting us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Security and privacy

  • Sign-in happens on each platform's own authorization page. We do not ask for, receive or store account passwords.
  • We request only the permissions listed above and use them only for the purposes described on this page.
  • The software runs on infrastructure operated by JCMS Digital Media, not on a shared third-party publishing service.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not sell personal information.
