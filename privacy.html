1. Who operates the Service

JCMS Social Publisher is operated by JCMS Digital Media ("JCMS", "we", "us"), [LEGAL ENTITY / BUSINESS TYPE – TO BE CONFIRMED]. We are the party that decides how the data described below is used. Contact details are in section 12.

2. Data we receive through authorization

When an account holder connects a social account, the platform sends us only the information covered by the permissions the account holder approved.

  • TikTok. With user.info.basic we receive the account's unique identifier for our app (open ID), display name and avatar. We use them only to show which account is connected and to direct videos to the right account. With video.upload and video.publish we can send videos to the account; these permissions do not let us read the account's private messages or password.
  • YouTube. We receive basic channel information, such as the channel name and identifier, needed to show which channel is connected and to upload videos to it. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Access tokens. The platform issues an access token (and, where provided, a refresh token) so that the Service can act on the account within the approved permissions without asking for a password.

We do not receive account passwords.

3. Videos and content

Videos, captions and settings are added to the Service by JCMS or by an authorized user. When a video is published or uploaded, the Service transmits the video file and caption to the platform you selected, using the permissions above. We use this content only to carry out that publishing action. We do not use your videos or account data for advertising, and we do not sell them.

4. What we store

To operate the Service, the Postiz software we run stores:

  • the identifier, display name and avatar of each connected account;
  • the access token and any refresh token issued for it;
  • videos, captions, schedules and publishing status that have been added to the Service; and
  • basic account details of authorized users of the Service.

We do not store the passwords of connected social accounts. We do not collect personal information through this website: it has no forms, accounts, analytics or advertising.

5. Where and for how long

Data is stored in the database and file storage of the Postiz installation operated by JCMS on infrastructure we control; it is not stored by a separate publishing vendor. We keep account connection data and tokens while an account remains connected. Content and publishing records are kept until we delete them or you ask us to. We have not set a fixed automatic deletion period. [RETENTION PERIOD – TO BE CONFIRMED BEFORE SUBMITTING]

6. Third parties

  • TikTok and Google/YouTube receive the videos and captions we publish to your accounts and process them under their own terms and privacy policies. They also provide the authorization we rely on.
  • Postiz is the open-source software we run. Because we operate it ourselves, Postiz's developers do not receive your data through our installation. [CONFIRM POSTIZ TELEMETRY SETTINGS – TO BE CONFIRMED]
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Protecting credentials

Account holders sign in on each platform's own page, so their passwords never reach us. Access tokens are held inside the Service's own database and are used only to publish within the approved permissions. We do not display them on this website. Access to the Service is limited to people JCMS authorizes. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. [CONFIRM TOKEN STORAGE AND ACCESS CONTROLS – TO BE CONFIRMED]

8. Disconnecting accounts

You can disconnect an account at any time by revoking the app's access in your account's settings on the platform (Google Account third-party access for YouTube; app permissions in your TikTok account), or by asking us to disconnect it. Once access is revoked or the account is disconnected, we stop publishing to it and remove its stored access tokens from the Service.

9. Deleting your data

To ask us to delete the data we hold about a connected account (identifier, display name, avatar, tokens, queued videos and publishing records), contact us using the details in section 12 and identify the account. We will confirm the request and delete the data from the Service, except where we must keep it by law. Content already published on TikTok or YouTube remains under those platforms' control; you can remove it there directly.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state privacy laws in the United States), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise a right, contact us using section 12. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy, for example when we add a platform. The date at the top shows the latest version.
